🚀 Getting Started
Is OneList free? ▼

Yes — completely free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

How do I create an account? ▼

Open the app, go to the Daily List or Profile tab and tap Sign In → Create Account. Enter an email and a password (at least 6 characters). That's it — your lists will now sync and can be shared.

How do I share a list with my partner? ▼
  • On your phone: create a list (the ＋ New List button on the Daily List tab).
  • Open the list → tap the ⓘ info icon → Share Invite Code, and send the 6-character code to your partner.
  • On their phone: they sign in with their own account, tap Join with Code, and enter the code.

You're now sharing the same list — changes appear on both phones live.

Do we need the same account? ▼

No. Each person signs in with their own email account. Sharing happens through the invite code, not a shared login.

⭐ Features
What's the difference between Daily List and Wishlist? ▼

Daily List is for shared shopping/to-do lists that sync live with the people you invite. Wishlist is for organizing things you want — with prices, store, priority and a monthly budget. Wishlists are stored on your device by default, and you can choose to make one shared too.

How does real-time sync work? ▼

When anyone on a shared list adds, edits or checks off an item, the change appears on every member's phone within a second or two (while the app is open). If you're offline, your changes are queued and sync automatically when you reconnect.

How do I add a product by sharing a link? ▼

In any shopping app or browser, use Share and pick OneList, or paste the link into the Add Item screen and tap Fetch Product Info. The app reads the page to fill in the product name, price and store automatically.

How do I add the home-screen widget? ▼
  • Long-press an empty area of your home screen → Widgets.
  • Find OneList and drag it onto the home screen.
  • Pick which list it should show.

You can then check items off right from the widget, and tap ＋ to open the list and add more. Add more than one widget for your other lists.

How do I lock a wishlist with a PIN? ▼

Set a 4-digit PIN under Profile → PIN Lock. Your PIN is stored only on your device as a secure one-way hash — it never leaves your phone and we can't see it.

Can I back up my wishlists? ▼

Yes. Go to Profile → Export Wishlists to save a JSON backup file, and Import Wishlists to restore it later or move it to another phone.

🔧 Troubleshooting
The widget shows "Can't load widget" ▼

This is a launcher caching hiccup that some phones (especially Xiaomi/MIUI) show when a widget is first added. To fix it:

  • Remove the widget and add it again, or
  • Restart your phone (or the launcher).

Once it loads the list once, it works normally after that.

Changes aren't syncing to the other phone ▼
  • Make sure both people are signed in and are members of the same list.
  • Check that both phones have an internet connection.
  • Open the list — sync resumes as soon as the app is open and online.
The invite code isn't working ▼

Codes are 6 characters and not case-sensitive. Make sure the person joining is signed in first, and double-check for easily-confused characters. If it still fails, ask the list owner to re-open the list info page and share the code again.

I forgot my PIN ▼

The PIN protects local wishlists and is stored only as a hash on your device, so it can't be recovered. You can clear it by reinstalling the app (note: this also clears local wishlists — export a backup first if you can).

🔒 Account & Privacy
Who can see my lists? ▼

A shared list is visible only to its members — the people who joined with its invite code. No one else, including other OneList users, can see it. Local wishlists stay on your device.

Do you show ads or sell my data? ▼

No. There are no ads, no analytics trackers, and we never sell or share your data with third parties. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my account and data? ▼

Open the app → Profile tab → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, the shared lists you own, and removes you from lists you joined.

You can also request deletion by email — see the account & data deletion page.
