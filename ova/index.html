Features

Track it. Then decide who sees it.

Most trackers are private or public. Ova is neither — you decide, person by person and category by category, and you can change your mind in one tap.

The dial

Where you are in your cycle, with the period, fertile window and ovulation drawn as shapes rather than colour alone.

Calendar and year view

Every day you logged, and a twelve-month grid where an irregular pattern becomes obvious in a way one month never shows.

Insights that answer something

Cycle length against the typical 21–35 day range, which symptoms show up most, and a summary in plain words.

Four modes

Trying to conceive, cycle awareness, pregnancy, or postpartum and baby. Switching changes what the app leads with, never what you logged.

Basal temperature

Read from Health Connect if a device records it, with the sustained rise that confirms ovulation marked on the curve.

Export for an appointment

A one-page PDF summary to take to a doctor, or the same thing as plain text. Both ask before anything leaves the app.

Sharing

Give your partner everything. Give your mother the dates.

  1. Create an invite

    Pick who it is for — partner, family, friend — and Ova sets sensible defaults you can change.

  2. Choose what it grants

    Cycle dates, symptoms, intimacy and mood are four separate switches. Each line says what it actually reveals, in plain words.

  3. Send it to one person

    They see only what you granted. Change it, pause it or end it at any time — removal takes effect immediately.

What we will not do

The parts most trackers leave vague

Predictions are never shared

Anyone you share with sees the days you logged, never Ova's estimate of your fertile window. That is enforced on the server, not hidden in the app.

Looking is visible both ways

You can see when each person last opened your data, and they can see when you opened theirs.

No ads, no analytics

There is no advertising SDK, no analytics and no crash reporting in the app. Nothing about how you use it is reported anywhere.

It is not contraception

Ova is not a medical device. Predictions are arithmetic on the dates you logged, and they are wrong more often the less you log.
