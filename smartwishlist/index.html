Features

Made for shopping together

Two halves in one app — a live shared shopping list, and a wishlist organizer for the things you're saving up for.

📝

Shared Daily List

Create a grocery or to-do list you both edit. Type and it's added; tap to check off when bought.

🔄

Real-time Sync

Anything either of you adds or checks off appears on the other phone within a second or two.

💞

Pair in Seconds

Create a list, share a 6-character invite code, and your partner joins — no complicated setup.

🎁

Wishlists

Organize the things you want — with prices, store, priority and notes. Keep a monthly budget in view.

🔗

Add by Sharing a Link

Share a product link from any shopping app and the details — name, price, store — fill in automatically.

🧩

Home-screen Widget

Put a list right on your home screen and check items off without even opening the app.

🔒

PIN Lock

Lock private wishlists behind a 4-digit PIN, stored only on your device as a secure hash.

🎨

Themes & Dark Mode

Five color palettes, light/dark mode, font and text-size options — make it yours.

📴

Works Offline

Wishlists live on your device; shared lists queue your changes offline and sync when you're back online.
