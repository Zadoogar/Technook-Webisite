Features

Everything you need to care from a distance

All features work silently in the background — your parents won't need to do a thing.

Live Location

See your parent's real-time location on a map. Request on-demand or enable live tracking at your chosen interval.

SOS Emergency

One tap on the SOS widget or 5 quick volume presses triggers an immediate alert with location to you.

Fall Detection

Automatic fall detection using the phone's accelerometer. Alerts you instantly if a fall is detected.

SMS Forwarding

Every SMS received on their phone — OTPs, bank alerts, delivery updates — forwarded to your dashboard.

Missed Call Alerts

Know when someone tried calling them and they missed it. Never let an important call go unnoticed.

Wellness Check

Send a gentle "are you okay?" check. Their phone shows a soft notification — one tap to confirm they're fine.

Notification Mirror

WhatsApp messages, bank alerts, UPI payments — see all their important app notifications in one place.

Daily Activity

Daily steps, screen time, app usage, sleep estimates — a gentle picture of their daily routine.

Works Offline Too

When their phone loses internet, SMS fallback kicks in automatically. Send commands via SMS, get replies.

How it works

Set up in minutes

  1. Install on both phones

    One app, two modes. Install FamilyGuard on your phone and your parent's phone.

  2. Set up Guardian mode

    On your parent's phone, sign in with Google and grant permissions. They see a consent screen explaining everything.

  3. Scan QR to link

    A QR code is generated. Scan it on your phone to pair the two devices securely.

  4. You're protected

    FamilyGuard runs silently in the background. Your dashboard is live.

Two modes

Two phones. One app.

Guardian Mode

Parent's phone — runs silently

  • Monitors in the background
  • Shares location when requested
  • Forwards SMS and calls
  • SOS widget on home screen
  • Fall detection always active
  • Mirrors app notifications
  • Battery & status reports

Watcher Mode

Your phone — full dashboard

  • Live map with location pin
  • SMS and calls feed
  • Push alerts for all events
  • Remote commands panel
  • Wellness check button
  • 7-day activity chart
  • Control parent device settings
