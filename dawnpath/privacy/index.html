Dawnpath Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Dawnpath ("the app") is a free debt tracker for Android, published by Technook ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: echoanup@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You create an account and type in your own debts. That is the data we hold.
  • It is stored in Google Firebase under your account so it follows you to a new phone.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.
  • Our service provider, Google Firebase, sees your IP address and basic device details when you sign in, for security.
  • We do not read your SMS, contacts or location, and we do not connect to your bank.
  • You can delete everything, including your account, from Settings at any time.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign up with email and password, we receive your email address. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and your name from your Google account. Firebase also creates a unique account ID for you.

Technical data. When you sign in or create an account, Firebase Authentication receives your IP address (used to keep sign-in secure and prevent abuse) and technical details about the app and device: the Firebase app ID and a user-agent string with the SDK version, Android version and device model. We do not use this for advertising or analytics.

What you enter. Loans, credit lines (credit cards, overdrafts, pay-later limits), family and friend IOUs, goals and payments: names, counterparty names you type, currencies, amounts, interest rates, instalments, dates, notes, your home currency and any exchange rates you enter. This is financial information about you, and you choose what to put in.

Not collected. Dawnpath does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, does not request location, contacts, SMS, photos or files, and does not use your advertising ID. Your IP address is not used by us to work out your location.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to store and show your debts, to calculate balances and projected payoff dates, and to send the reminders you ask for. Payoff dates are projections based on what you enter. They are not financial advice.

Where it is stored, and who handles it

Your account and your entries are stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC, which processes the data for us as a service provider. Data travels over encrypted connections (TLS). Google may process data in countries other than your own; see Google's Firebase privacy information at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

On your phone

  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server for them. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android settings.
  • Widgets show a small snapshot (your debt-free date, totals, this month's payments) kept on your device so the home screen can draw it. You can hide the amounts in Settings, and the snapshot is cleared when you delete your account.
  • Settings such as theme, home currency and your reminder and widget choices are stored on your device.
  • Android may include these on-device files in your device backup if you have backup turned on.

Advertising

Dawnpath has no ads today. If we ever add ads or analytics, we will update this policy and the app's Play Store data-safety information before we do.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, loans, payments, credit lines and their entries (including any older card records), goals and settings stored in Firestore, and then your sign-in account. Encrypted backups kept by Google may take up to about 180 days to be fully overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete in the app: Settings > Delete account.
  • Delete without the app: follow https://technook.in/dawnpath/delete-account/ (the account deletion page) or email echoanup@gmail.com from the address on your account.
  • Access or correct your data by opening the app; everything we hold is what you can see there. You may also email us to ask for a copy.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR, or in California), you may have further rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Dawnpath is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email echoanup@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for material changes tell you in the app or by email before they apply.
