Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective immediately

The short version: OneList stores your account (email + display name) and the lists you create so they can sync and be shared with people you invite. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't share your data with anyone except the list members you choose. You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

1. Who We Are

OneList is an Android application developed by Technook. It is a shared shopping-list and wishlist app for couples and families.

Developer: Technook (Anup Kumar) · Delhi, India
Website: technook.in
Contact: echoanup@gmail.com

2. How the App Works

OneList has two parts:

  • Shared lists — daily/grocery lists you share with people you invite. These are stored in the cloud (Google Firebase) so they update live on every member's phone.
  • Wishlists — your personal list of things you want, stored on your device. You may optionally make a wishlist shared, which then syncs like a shared list.

An account (email + password) is required to create or join shared lists, so your data can sync between devices and be shared with the people you invite.

3. Data We Collect

We only collect what's needed to make the app work. We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or any advertising identifiers.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To sync your lists across your devices and the devices of members you invite
  • To show who added or checked off each item on a shared list
  • Data on a shared list is visible only to the members of that list
  • We never sell or rent your data, and never share it with advertisers
  • We use no advertising SDKs and run no third-party analytics or tracking

5. Data Storage & Security

Cloud storage: Shared lists and your basic profile (email + display name) are stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Security rules ensure a list's data can only be read or changed by members of that list.

On-device storage: Local wishlists and settings are stored in a private database on your phone. Your PIN is stored only as a SHA-256 hash on the device.

In transit: All communication with Firebase is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS.

Authentication: Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your raw password.

8. Deleting Your Account & Data

You can permanently delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time:

  • Open the app → Profile tab → Delete account
  • This deletes your profile, the shared lists you own, and removes you from lists you joined
  • It cannot be undone

Prefer to request it by email, or can't access the app? See our account & data deletion page, or email echoanup@gmail.com and we'll delete your data within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all local data (your on-device wishlists and settings) from your phone.

9. Your Rights

  • Access: Your data is visible to you inside the app; wishlists can be exported to a JSON file
  • Correction: Edit your display name, lists and items at any time in the app
  • Deletion: Delete your account and data in-app, or request it by email
  • Leave a shared list: You can leave any shared list at any time, which removes you from it

10. Children's Privacy

OneList is a general-audience utility app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app. Continued use of OneList means you accept the updated policy.
