How can we help?

Answers to the things people ask most.

Getting started
Is Ova free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no paid tier. There is nothing in the app that makes money from your data.

Why does it only offer Google sign-in?

So your data can reach your other devices and so a share can point at a real account. Ova never sees your Google password, and it does not store your email address.

Do I have to log anything to use it?

No. The app works with nothing logged — it just cannot tell you much. Predictions get better the more you log, and worse the less you do.

Sharing
What exactly does someone see when I share with them?

Only the categories you granted, and only the days you logged. They never see predictions or your fertile window — that is refused by the server, not just hidden in the app.

Can I change my mind after sharing?

Yes. You can change which categories a person sees, pause them, or remove them entirely. Removal takes effect immediately.

Can they tell that I looked at their data?

Yes, and you can tell when they looked at yours. Looking is visible in both directions, on purpose.

What happens if I send an invite link to the wrong person?

The first person to open it can accept it. If you think a link went astray, remove that person from your Circle — access ends immediately.

Basal temperature
I connected Health Connect but nothing was imported.

Most watches and bands record skin temperature, which is a different measurement. Ova reads only true basal body temperature readings, so if your device records the other kind there is nothing for it to import. That is the device, not a fault in the app.

Is my temperature shared with anyone?

Never. It is not offered as a category when you invite someone, and the server refuses to serve it to a viewer under any configuration. A temperature curve says whether you ovulated, which is a conclusion about your body rather than a date.

Privacy and data
Is my data end-to-end encrypted?

No, and we would rather say so than let you assume otherwise. It is encrypted in transit and stored in a form the service can read. Ova does not read it and access is enforced by server-side rules, but a policy is not the same as cryptography.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete account, inside the app. It removes the copy on the server too, and ends every share the moment you confirm. Full details on the delete account page.

Can I use this as contraception?

No. Ova is not contraception and not a medical device. Predictions are arithmetic on the dates you logged. Anything that looks like a health concern is a conversation for a doctor.

Still stuck?

Email us and we will get back to you. Please say which phone and Android version you are on — it saves a round trip.
