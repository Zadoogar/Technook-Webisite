Delete Your Account & Data

Everything, including the copy on the server. From inside the app.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Ova and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to Delete account, at the bottom of the data group.
  3. Read the confirmation — it names the people who lose access.
  4. Tap Delete everything.
This is permanent and cannot be undone. It is not the same as signing out — signing out leaves everything on the server.

What gets deleted

  • Every period, flow, symptom, mood and intimacy log
  • Basal temperature readings imported from Health Connect
  • Postpartum feed, sleep and nappy counts
  • The copy on the server, not just the one on your phone
  • Every share — anyone you shared with loses access the moment you confirm
  • Every share you were following — you stop seeing their data
  • Pending invites you created
  • Your profile and the sign-in account itself

Access is revoked before anything is deleted. If the deletion is interrupted — you lose connection halfway — it never leaves your data readable by someone who was part-way removed.

What deleting does not touch

Anything you exported yourself. A PDF you saved or a summary you sent to someone is outside Ova once it leaves the app, and deleting your account cannot reach it.

Data belonging to people who shared with you stays theirs. Deleting your account removes your access to it, not the data.
