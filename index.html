How we build

What we won't do

Most of what makes an app annoying is a business decision, not a technical one. These are ours.

No ads. Ever.

Not a banner between your groceries, not a full-screen one when you open the app. There is nothing to dismiss.

No paywall at item 21

No trial that expires, no "upgrade to add more". If a feature exists, it works from the first minute.

Your data isn't the product

We don't share your data with third parties. Your lists are yours; we have no reason to look at them.

Built for patchy signal

Basement aisle with no bars? Add items anyway. Everything queues up and syncs the moment you're back online.

Made for Indian homes

Atta, dal, rice and sugar are one tap, not a search. Available in Hindi and seven other languages.

Tested on real phones

On the mid-range Android handsets people actually own, with real family data — not just a clean emulator.

Get in touch

Something broken? An idea?

Bug reports, feature requests and collaborations all reach the same inbox — and get read by the person who wrote the code.
