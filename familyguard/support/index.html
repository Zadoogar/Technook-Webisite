🚀 Getting Started
How do I set up FamilyGuard? ▼

Install FamilyGuard on both phones — your phone and your parent's phone.

  • On your parent's phone: Open the app, select "Guardian" mode, sign in with Google, complete the consent screen, and grant permissions. A QR code will be generated.
  • On your phone: Open the app, select "Watcher" mode, sign in with Google, and scan the QR code shown on your parent's phone.

That's it! The two devices are now paired and FamilyGuard is active.

Do both phones need the same Google account? ▼

No — each phone uses a different Google account. Your parent signs in with their own Google account on their phone, and you sign in with your own Google account on your phone.

What if my parent doesn't have a Google account? ▼

A Google account is required to use FamilyGuard (it's needed for secure authentication and Firebase). You can create a Google account for your parent during setup — it takes just a few minutes and just requires a name and a password.

Can I re-install the app without losing the pairing? ▼

Yes. If you uninstall and reinstall the app and sign in with the same Google account, the pairing is automatically restored — no QR scan needed. This works for both Guardian and Watcher devices.

⭐ Features
How do I request my parent's location? ▼

On your Watcher dashboard, tap the "Request Location" button on the Home tab. Their phone will fetch a fresh GPS fix and send it to your map within 10 seconds.

For continuous tracking, go to Profile → Parent Device Settings → set the interval → tap "Apply Live Tracking".

How does the SOS work? ▼

There are two ways to trigger SOS from the Guardian (parent) device:

  • Widget button: Tap the red SOS button on their home screen
  • Volume shortcut: Press the volume down button 5 times quickly (within 3 seconds)

When triggered, you receive: a push notification, an SMS with a Google Maps link, and the Guardian phone auto-dials your number.

What is the Wellness Check? ▼

The Wellness Check lets you send a gentle "are you okay?" check to your parent without calling them.

From your dashboard, tap "Wellness Check". Their phone shows a soft notification saying "Your family is checking on you 💙" with an "I'm Okay ✓" button. When they tap it, you see a green confirmation. If they don't respond within the timeout period, you receive an alert.

How does fall detection work? ▼

FamilyGuard uses the phone's built-in accelerometer to detect falls. It looks for a sudden impact (the phone accelerating rapidly) followed by a period of stillness — the pattern of a fall.

You can adjust the fall sensitivity in Profile → Parent Device Settings. If you get false alerts, increase the sensitivity threshold. The default setting works well for most situations.

How do remote commands work? ▼

From your Watcher dashboard, you can send commands that execute on your parent's phone within seconds:

  • Request Location — get their current GPS
  • Ring Phone — make their phone ring loudly even on silent
  • Torch ON/OFF — control the flashlight
  • Volume controls — adjust their phone volume remotely
  • Call Me — their phone auto-dials your number
🔧 Troubleshooting
SMS messages are not being forwarded ▼

This is usually a background service issue. Try these steps:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → FamilyGuard → Battery → set to "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions"
  • On Xiaomi/MIUI: Settings → Apps → FamilyGuard → enable Auto-start
  • On Samsung: Device Care → Battery → App power management → set FamilyGuard to "Unrestricted"
  • Make sure the SMS permission is granted: Settings → Apps → FamilyGuard → Permissions → SMS → Allow
Location is not updating / REQUEST LOCATION doesn't work ▼

Check these settings on the parent's phone:

  • Settings → Apps → FamilyGuard → Permissions → Location → set to "Allow all the time" (not just "While using the app")
  • Make sure GPS/Location is turned ON in the phone settings
  • Battery optimization must be disabled for FamilyGuard (see above)
I'm not receiving push notifications ▼

Check these on your Watcher phone:

  • Settings → Apps → FamilyGuard → Notifications → make sure all channels are enabled
  • Check that notification preferences in Profile → App Customisations are not turned off
  • Try opening the app once — this refreshes the FCM token
  • If using battery saver mode, it can delay notifications — disable for FamilyGuard
The app stops working after some time on Xiaomi / MIUI ▼

MIUI aggressively kills background apps. You must do all of the following:

  • Settings → Apps → Manage apps → FamilyGuard → Battery saver → No restrictions
  • Settings → Apps → Manage apps → FamilyGuard → enable Autostart
  • Settings → Battery & performance → App battery saver → FamilyGuard → No restrictions
  • In recent apps, long-press FamilyGuard and lock it (padlock icon)

After making these changes, restart the phone and test again.

How do I unlink the devices? ▼

On the Watcher phone: go to Profile → scroll to the bottom → tap "Unlink Guardian" → confirm. Both devices will be unlinked and return to the setup screen. All local data will be cleared.

🔒 Privacy & Data
Does my parent know the app is installed? ▼

Yes, absolutely. FamilyGuard requires:

  • Physical setup on the parent's phone — it cannot be installed remotely
  • The parent to sign in with their own Google account
  • The parent to read and accept a full consent screen before setup continues

Additionally, a persistent notification is always visible when the app is running, and the app icon is always visible in the launcher. FamilyGuard is designed for use with the parent's full knowledge and consent — it is not a spy app.

Who can see the data? ▼

Only the single linked family member (Watcher) can see the data. Firebase security rules ensure that no other account — not even other FamilyGuard users — can access your family's data.

How long is data stored? ▼

Data in Firebase (the cloud) is automatically deleted after 24 hours. The Watcher's phone keeps a local cache in its own database. You can export alert history as a PDF from the Alerts tab before it's deleted.

How do I delete all my data? ▼

Uninstalling the app stops all data collection immediately. Firebase data auto-deletes after 24 hours. For immediate complete deletion of all Firebase data, contact us at the email below and we'll delete it manually.
