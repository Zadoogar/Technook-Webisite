Features

Made for shopping together

OneList 2.0 is a fresh start: a warm new design, real photos for everyday items, and a home for every list you keep together, from the weekly shop to birthday gifts.

Shop together, live

Changes show on everyone's phone the moment you make them. Every item shows who added it, and OneList tells you when something is already on the list.

Invite with a 6-letter code

No phone numbers and no access to your contacts. Use it without an account; sign in with Google or email when you want to share.

Built for the shop

Items sort themselves into aisles. Buy again keeps your regulars one tap away, and you can add many items at once by voice or paste a list from a message.

Store mode

Big rows, the screen stays on and done items fold away. See when your partner is shopping so you don't both go, and finish the trip to see who got what.

Wishlists with price drops

Save things from any shop by sharing the link to OneList. Price-drop alerts tell you when something gets cheaper, and a monthly budget shows what fits.

Gift lists

A list for someone's birthday, with a countdown. Family can quietly claim gifts, so nobody buys the same present twice and who's getting what stays a surprise.

Right where you need it

A home-screen widget to tick items off without opening the app, app shortcuts, an activity feed, and quieter notifications with quiet hours.

Yours

Light and dark, five accent colours or your wallpaper's colours, and a PIN lock for private lists.

Works offline

Add items in a basement aisle with no signal. Everything syncs when you're back, and you can export and import your data.

How it works

Up and running in a minute

  1. Open the app

    Start a list straight away, no account needed. Sign in with Google or email when you want to share.

  2. Create a list

    Start a shared list — "Grocery", "Shopping", anything you both need.

  3. Share the invite code

    Send the 6-character code to your partner. They sign in and join with it.

  4. Shop together

    Add, edit and check off items — every change shows up live on both phones.
