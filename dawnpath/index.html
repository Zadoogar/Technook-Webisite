Features

See the day you're debt-free.

Add what you owe and what you pay. Your Journey shows a projected debt-free date, how much you've paid and how far is left — and the date moves the moment a payment changes.

Everything in one place

Home, car, personal, education and gold loans, pay-later plans and informal IOUs. Interest, EMIs and due dates in 150+ currencies, with totals in your home currency.

Credit lines, on their own tab

Cards, app credit lines, overdrafts and pay-later limits. See what you borrowed and what it cost in interest — and a plan to clear the line on a monthly budget.

Money you've lent

What family and friends owe you sits beside what you owe, so nothing slips your mind.

Assumed paid — but we ask

Regular payments count as paid so you don't log each one. Every so often Dawnpath asks you to confirm: one tap for paid, one for missed.

Snowball or avalanche

Scenarios shows how much sooner you'd be debt-free, and how much interest you'd save, by paying extra each month — highest rate first or smallest balance first.

Reminders and widgets

A gentle nudge before a payment is due, and home-screen widgets for your Journey, a debt-free countdown or this month's payments — amounts hideable in one setting.

Private by design

You type it in. Nothing reads it for you.

Never reads your SMS

Many money apps scan your messages for bank alerts. Dawnpath doesn't ask for SMS access at all, and never connects to your bank.

Free. No ads. No in-app purchases.

Every feature works from the first minute. There is no premium tier to unlock the debt-free date.

Delete it all, any time

Your data is stored under your own account. Delete it, and the account itself, from Settings whenever you like.

A tracker, not a lender

Dawnpath doesn't lend money or arrange loans. Payoff dates are projections from what you enter, not financial advice.
