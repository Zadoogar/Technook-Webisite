The questions that actually come up

Health Connect

The health screen is empty

Three things to check, in this order:

  • Is Health Connect installed? On Android 13 and earlier it is a separate app from the Play Store. On Android 14 and later it is built into Settings.
  • Has anything written to it? Health Connect is a store, not a source. It stays empty until a watch, a phone's step counter, a smart scale or another fitness app writes to it. GymTrackr only reads.
  • Are the permissions granted? Settings › Health Connect inside GymTrackr shows how many are on.

I only want to share some of it

Every permission is separate. Grant steps and refuse sleep if you like — the cards you did not grant simply do not appear, and nothing else changes.

Does GymTrackr change my health data?

Only body weight, and only when you log one. Everything else is read-only. Full detail is on the privacy page.

Your data

Where is it stored?

On your phone, and only on your phone. GymTrackr has no account and no internet permission, so there is no copy on a server anywhere.

This cuts both ways. Nobody can read your training data, and nobody can restore it for you either. If you lose your phone without a backup file, it is gone.

Backing up

Settings › Back up & restore › Export writes everything to a single file. Keep it somewhere that is not the same phone. The screen shows the date of your last export so you can see at a glance whether you are overdue.

Moving to a new phone

Export on the old phone, install GymTrackr on the new one, then Settings › Back up & restore › Restore and pick the file. Do the export before you wipe the old device.

Deleting everything

Settings › Delete all data clears it immediately, or just uninstall the app. There is no account to close and nothing to email us about.

Training

An exercise I do is not in the list

Add it: the exercise picker has "Create a custom exercise" at the bottom. Pick its equipment and the muscles it works — those drive the body map and your weekly coverage, so choose the muscles the lift actually trains rather than everything it touches.

My weights are in pounds

Settings › Units. You can also set the plate increment there, so the plus and minus buttons step by the smallest plate you actually own.

The rest timer keeps starting on its own

That is deliberate — it starts when you tick a set so it cannot be forgotten. Skip dismisses it, and it never covers the sets you are reading.
