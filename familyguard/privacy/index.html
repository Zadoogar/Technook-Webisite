Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Plain language summary: FamilyGuard helps family members monitor elderly parents with their full knowledge and consent. Your data is shared only with your linked family member — never sold, never shared with third parties, auto-deleted after 24 hours.

1. Who We Are

FamilyGuard is an Android application developed by Technook, available on the Google Play Store. The app helps adult children remotely monitor the safety and wellbeing of their elderly parents.

Developer: Technook (Anup Kumar) · Delhi, India
Website: technook.in
Contact: echoanup@gmail.com

2. How the App Works

FamilyGuard operates in two modes within a single app:

  • Guardian mode — installed on the parent's phone. Collects and shares safety data with consent.
  • Watcher mode — installed on the family member's phone. Receives and displays the shared data.

The parent must complete a full consent screen during setup before any data collection begins. Setup requires physical access to the parent's device — the app cannot be installed or activated remotely.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Data is shared exclusively with the single linked family member (Watcher)
  • Data is stored in Firebase Firestore and automatically deleted after 24 hours
  • Data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or any third parties
  • Data is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics beyond app functionality
  • Location is only collected when requested or during SOS/geofence events
  • App usage and notification data only collected when permission is explicitly granted

5. Data Storage & Security

Storage: All data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore. Data is automatically deleted after 24 hours. A local cache is maintained on the Watcher's device using Android Room database.

Access control: Firestore security rules ensure only the Guardian's device can write data, and only the linked Watcher can read it. No other account can access the data.

Authentication: All access requires Google Sign-In. Device pairing requires physical QR code scan — remote pairing is impossible.

Transmission: All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections.

7. Consent & Transparency

  • Full consent screen shown during Guardian setup before any data collection begins
  • Consent screen lists every type of data collected — no surprises
  • Guardian must actively tap "I Understand & Accept" — no default opt-in
  • App icon always visible in phone launcher — the app cannot hide itself
  • Persistent notification always visible when monitoring service is running
  • Guardian can unlink from family member at any time from in-app settings
  • Consent timestamp is logged to Firebase for audit purposes

8. Your Rights

  • Access: View all data stored about your device in the Guardian app settings
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app stops all data collection. Firebase data auto-deletes after 24 hours. Contact us for immediate full deletion.
  • Withdrawal: The Guardian can unlink at any time, immediately stopping all data sharing
  • Export: Alert history can be exported as a PDF from the Watcher app

10. Children's Privacy

FamilyGuard is intended for adults (18+) only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used this app, contact us immediately for data deletion.
