Delete your Dawnpath account

App: Dawnpath: Debt Payoff Tracker (com.technook.dawnpath), by Technook.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Dawnpath and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account ("Erases every debt, credit line and payment").
  4. Read the warning and tap Delete forever.

If Dawnpath says it needs a recent sign-in, it signs you out. Sign in again and repeat steps 2 to 4.

Option 2: by email, without the app

Email echoanup@gmail.com from the address on your Dawnpath account (for a Google sign-in, your Google email), with the subject Delete my Dawnpath account. We may reply to confirm it is you. We will delete the account within 30 days of confirming, usually much sooner, and let you know when done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, name and account ID)
  • Your profile, home currency and exchange rates
  • All loans, payments, credit lines, their entries (and any older card records) and goals you entered
  • The widget snapshot on your phone (cleared when you delete in the app; you can also remove the widgets or uninstall the app)

What is kept

Nothing about your account is kept after deletion. Copies in Google's encrypted backups can take up to about 180 days to be fully overwritten and are not used for any other purpose. We may keep minimal records only if the law requires it.

Timeline

  • In the app: immediate.
  • By email: within 30 days of confirming your identity.
Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or data; use one of the options above first.
