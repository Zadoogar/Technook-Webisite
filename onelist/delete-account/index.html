Delete Your Account & Data

You can remove your account and data at any time.

Delete from inside the app

The fastest way is right in OneList:

  1. Open OneList and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account (at the bottom of the list).
  4. Confirm, and re-enter your password when asked.
This is permanent and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email login) and your profile / display name
  • The shared lists you own, including their items
  • Your membership in any lists you joined (you're removed from them)
  • Local data on your phone — your on-device wishlists, settings and PIN — is removed when you uninstall the app

Note: shared lists owned by someone else continue to exist for their other members; deleting your account simply removes you from them.

Prefer to request it by email?

If you can't access the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your account and data for you:
